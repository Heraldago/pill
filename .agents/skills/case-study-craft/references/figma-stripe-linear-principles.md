## 1. Portfolio & Case Study Advice (Figma Product Design Team)

- **Lead with Your Best & Most Natural Story**: Open with the project you can speak to most naturally with a clear problem statement, milestones, and conclusion.
- **Depth Over Breadth (2-3 Deep Projects + Optional B-Reel)**: A few deep case studies beat many shallow ones. Keep exploratory visual candy in a separate "B-reel" or archive if desired.
- **"Why" Over "What" (Trade-offs)**:
  - *Tammy Taabassum*: "Why you did something is often more important than what you did because 'why' showcases your product thinking. Every color, component, style, bit of copy is a choice, and being able to articulate what trade-offs you considered and the pros and cons of those choices makes it clear that you understand nuances, constraints, and the implications of different options."
- **Unshipped & Exploratory Work**:
  - *Julia Han*: Don't limit case studies to shipped features. Showing exploratory designs, discarded iterations, and future visions reveals how you think beyond requirements.
- **Bring Work to Life (GIFs & Video Walkthroughs)**:
  - Static mocks aren't enough. Use recordings, GIFs, and interactive prototypes so reviewers immediately grasp interaction flows and motion craft.
- **Meticulous Finer Details Over Risky Flourishes**:
  - *Chia Amisola*: "Being intentional about these finer details [meta tags, grids, spacing, typography, punctuation, responsive layouts] stands out more than trying to differentiate with riskier personality flourishes."
  - Avoid password barriers whenever possible.
- **Own Failures & Pivots (Noah Levin & Sara Culver)**:
  - Hiring loops (recruiters, managers, peers, eng/PM partners) look for self-awareness. Sharing a failed experiment or mid-sprint pivot and how you adapted proves maturity.

## 2. Craft, Beauty, and Business ROI (Stripe, Linear, Figma)

- **Aesthetic-Usability Effect & Conversion (Katie Dill, Stripe)**:
  - Beauty is not cosmetic; clarity, copy hierarchy, and visual craft make products easier to understand and succeed with (e.g., +11.9% revenue on Stripe Optimized Checkout, +20% conversion on redesigned email series).
- **Craft is the Mindset; Quality is the Output (Karri Saarinen, Linear)**:
  - Craft is caring about how the window opens and closes, not just completing a ticket.
  - Validate quality through both metrics and "rave reviews" (anecdotal love, champions, word-of-mouth). Product quality is the ultimate moat.
- **Hierarchy of Needs & 60fps Foundation (Yuhki Yamashita, Figma)**:
  - Performance, frame rate (60fps), and intuitive responsiveness are table stakes before higher-level features matter.
- **Friction Logging / "Walking the Store" (Katie Dill, Stripe)**:
  - Audit the end-to-end user journey across every touchpoint like a real user to spot where value is added or subtracted.

## 3. Collaborative Systems & Human Context (Config 2020 Keynote)

- **Multi-player Collaboration (Dylan Field)**: Design happens in open, cross-functional loops with engineering and stakeholders, not in isolation.
- **Tangible Constraints & Documentation (Craig Mod)**: Grounding digital iterations in real-world human context, physical observation, and disciplined documentation.
- **Systems + Soul (Devon Zuegel, May-Li Khoe, Scott Hansen)**: Modular design systems provide structure, while play, curiosity, and emotional resonance make the product memorable.
